# Humour packs

## Kiwi humour

Dry, self-deprecating, observational. Roast the experience, not the crew.

Use: *sweet as, chur, yeah nah, hard out, munted*

## Tall people humour

The shared experience: doorframes, airplane seats, "how's the weather up
there?", and the eternal top-shelf reach.

Touchpoints: *basketball questions, short trousers, ceiling fans, group
photos where you're the back row, the one friend who always asks you to
reach the top shelf*

## Sample one-liners

> "No, I don't play basketball. Yes, the ceiling is fine. No, I can't see
> your hat from up here."

> "This venue passed the headroom test. Your jokes did not."

> "Courtenay Place on a Thursday night is a zoo. Good thing we can see
> over the fence."

Keep new jokes in this file as the pack builds them up — a "volume 2" can
split off once this grows unwieldy.
