## The Meetup Broadcast

Run on Instagram Broadcast Channel + Stories:

| Time | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 3 days out | 📍 Venue revealed. Headroom confirmed. |
| 2 days out | What we're ordering — and the table plan. |
| 1 day out | WHO'S IN? Drop a 🦒. |
| Day of | Table's booked. Save us the end seat. |
| After | Recap post — who came, what we drank. |

## Content pillars (owned)

| Pillar | Frequency | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
