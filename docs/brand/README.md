# Brand voice & guidelines

**Brand name:** Tall Poppies Pōneke
**Tagline:** *Standing tall in Pōneke.*

**Concept:** A community-driven, unfiltered, matey social club for tall
people in Wellington (NZ). Regular meetups — pub nights, walks, coffee —
at venues with headroom, run by the crew that books the back table.
Targeting tall locals (20s–40s) and anyone who has ever been asked
"how's the weather up there?".

**Mission:** To give the tall people of Pōneke a regular, low-fuss social —
good venues, good chat, no ducking.

**Vision:** To become Wellington's most trusted, matey meetup for the long
and the lofty.

## Voice & personality

**Archetype:** The Friendly Giant / The Good Host
**Tone:** Warm, dry, self-aware, reliably matey.
**Vibe:** *"The mate who checks the ceiling height before booking, saves
you the end seat so you can stretch your legs, and never asks the obvious
question."*

**Three pillars:**

- **Headroom-first** — venues vetted for space, light, and table fit
- **Crowd-led** — members pick the spots; the crew shows up
- **Local-first** — Pōneke venues over chains, every time

## Grammar & tone

- Sentence case (never ALL CAPS)
- Short, punchy sentences
- Ellipses for lines, em-dashes for asides
- Max 3 emojis per caption: 🦒 🍻 ☕ 📍
- Hashtags: `#TallPoppiesPōneke` + `#PōnekeSocials` + one rotating tag
