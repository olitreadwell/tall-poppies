# Pōneke social meetup landscape — competitor audit

| Competitor | What they do | The gap we fill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meetup.com | General interest groups, fees, clunky | Free, local, tall-specific |
| Facebook Groups | Chaotic event posts | Structured, consistent, scannable |
| Sports clubs | Team-based, competitive | Social-first, no commitment |
| Church / community groups | Regular but niche | No agenda, just good chat |
| Venue socials | Self-promotional events | Crew-led, honest venue checks |

## Positioning

> "Meetup.com is for networking. Sports clubs are for competition. Tall
> Poppies Pōneke is for **you** — the tall local who wants a regular,
> low-fuss social with people who finally get it, at venues with actual
> headroom."
