## Fix these first

1. The event says 5pm to 8pm. It needs to say 5pm to 8pm. Edit the event, not the page.

## Profile and cover

- Profile image: `assets/png/fb-profile.png`. It is 640 by 640 and displays as a circle, so keep the mark centred.
- Cover image: `assets/png/fb-page-cover.png`, 1640 by 624. Facebook crops the sides on mobile. Keep every word inside the middle 640 pixels.
- Do not put the date on the cover. It goes stale the day after the meetup and you will forget to change it.

## Bio, 255 characters

## About

Two paragraphs. Facebook hides anything after the first three lines behind See more, so the date goes in the opening line.

Tall People Meetup Wellington is a monthly social for tall people in the city, plus their mates, partners and anyone else who wants a low-fuss drink with people who fit.

Next meetup: Wednesday 25 November, 5pm to 8pm, Rosie's Red-Hot Cantina, 4 Queens Wharf, Wellington. Free to attend, no membership, no sign up.

As far as the internet can tell, this is Wellington's first tall people meetup. The worldwide scene is real. Wellington just got left short.

We also back tall-advantaged sport in the city. Basketball, netball, volleyball. If your club wants a loud crowd, get in touch.

All welcome. Come along. If you don't quite qualify, bring a tall friend.

### 2. Why we exist

Finally, an event where you won't automatically be the tallest person in the room.

Come meet your vertical peers and swap notes on the things that actually matter. Surviving a long-haul flight when your knees are already against the seat in front. Group photo poses that work when your mate is 160cm. The quiet guilt of blocking the view at a concert.

Our totally unofficial definition of tall: women 5'10" and over, men 6'2" and over.

Everyone is welcome. If you don't quite qualify, invite someone who does.

Wednesday 25 November, 5pm to 8pm, Rosie's Red-Hot Cantina, 4 Queens Wharf.

## Also make a group

A page broadcasts. A group is where the meetup actually lives between events.

Create a linked group called Tall People Meetup Wellington Chat. Set it to private, ask two questions to join, and link it from the page. The page posts the dates. The group carries the conversation, the lift shares and the photos afterwards.

## Before you publish

- Add both organisers as page admins, not just one.
- Turn on message replies. Set a saved reply for "when is it" with the date, time and venue.
- Check the page on a phone. Most of your audience will only see the mobile crop.
- Do not buy a page like or a boost until the page has the event, the cover and three posts on it.
