# Coline's club outreach email

Coline sends this from her own address. It goes to club admins, coaches and social secretaries, not to a mailing list.

**Send it as a draft.** Do not bulk send. Clubs get a lot of this and one personal line beats twenty identical emails.

## Who to send it to

Basketball, netball and volleyball clubs around Wellington first. Then rowing, swimming and athletics clubs, because tall people drift there too.

Find the contact on the club website, usually under Contact or Committee. Use a person's name if one is listed. If the only address is `info@`, send the short version.

Fill in before sending:

- `[First name]` if you have it, otherwise drop the greeting and start with "Kia ora,"
- `[Club name]`
- `[event link]`

## Version 1: email

**Subject:** Tall People Meetup, 25 Nov. Know anyone?

Kia ora [First name],

I'm Coline, one of the organisers of Tall People Meetup Wellington. We're a new social group for tall people in the city and our first meetup is on Wednesday 25 November, 5pm to 8pm, at Rosie's Red-Hot Cantina on Queens Wharf.

I'm writing to [Club name] because your sport is one of the few places where being tall is an outright advantage, and we'd rather be useful to that world than just drink near it.

The plan is for the meetup to turn up and support tall-advantaged sport in Wellington. That means coming along to basketball, netball and volleyball games, making some noise, and putting tall people in touch with clubs that actually want them.

Two things would help, whichever is easier:

- Forward this to anyone in your club who might want to come along.
- Or reply with a name and I'll get in touch directly.

No cost, no membership, nothing to sign up for. Everyone is welcome, tall or not. If someone doesn't quite qualify, they should bring a tall friend.

Wednesday 25 November, 5pm to 8pm, Rosie's Red-Hot Cantina, 4 Queens Wharf, Wellington.

Event page: [event link]

Thanks,

Coline
Tall People Meetup Wellington

## Follow up once

If there is no reply after three days, send one line:

> Kia ora [First name], just floating this back up in case it got buried. Meetup is Wednesday 25 November, 5pm, Rosie's on Queens Wharf. Happy to send a ready-made post if that helps. Thanks, Coline.

Then stop. Do not send a third.

## Notes

- Reply to every yes within a day. A club that gets a reply in an hour will share the next one too.
- If a club offers to post it, send them the finished square image and the caption as plain text, ready to paste.
- If a club offers a player to come along, that is worth more than a share. Say yes and get a name.
- Keep the sports claim to support and attendance. Do not imply sponsorship, funding or affiliation until someone has actually agreed to it.
