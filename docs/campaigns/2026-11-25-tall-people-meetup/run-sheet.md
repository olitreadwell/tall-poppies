# Tall People Meetup: ideas for the night

Wednesday 25 November 2026, 5pm to 8pm, Rosie's Red-Hot Cantina, 4 Queens Wharf.

Written for Coline. Read it once, pick what helps, ignore the rest. None of
this is a checklist.

## Five things worth doing

- Confirm the booking and the reserved area.
- Put one person at the door to greet.
- Put name tags and markers on a small table.
- Make one short welcome around 5:30 or 6:00.
- Thank the venue by name before you leave.

## Ten minutes before

- Check the event page says 5pm to 8pm.
- Charge your phone.
- Print two signs, one for the door and one for the bar.
- Pack markers, tape and a power bank.
- Ask staff for a name and number.

## Setup, if it is easy

- Put the door sign at eye level.
- Put the second sign near the bar.
- Put the tag table beside the entrance, with one chair.
- Put water and glasses on the table.
- Ask staff to keep the music low.
- Check the floor for cables.

## When people arrive

- Stand where you can see the door, and greet each person within 30 seconds.
- Offer a name tag. Ask nothing about height.
- Introduce a newcomer to one person.
- Explain the night: 5pm to 8pm, come and go. Give the photo rule before any photo.
- Point out the drinks and the quiet corner. Watch for anyone at the edge.

## In the middle

- Welcome once: who you are and thanks to the venue.
- Ask for a show of hands: first meetup or returning.
- Run one icebreaker for 10 minutes at most, if it feels right.
- Move around every 15 minutes.
- Open a closed circle by introducing someone.
- Take a group photo around 6:30, with consent.

## Near the end

- Say the end time at 7:30.
- Thank people as they leave.
- Ask one question: what should the next one do?
- Take down the signs and check for lost property.
- Thank the venue by name.

## Optional extras, pick two at most

- Conversation cards: travel, clothes, desks and venue headroom.
- Venue bingo: headroom, legroom, coat hook, accessible toilet.
- Buddy pairing for first-timers.
- A quiet table with no standing.
- A sign-up sheet for the next meetup.

## Photo rule

Ask before a close-up. Ask before posting a face. A wide room shot with no
clear subject is usually fine.

If someone says no, do not take it. If someone asks you to delete a photo,
delete it.

## After

- Post a thank-you story the same night, if you have the energy.
- Write down attendance and one quote.
- Pick the next date within two days.

## If the night goes sideways

- Food is late: tell people where to order.
- Music is loud: ask staff once.
- Someone looks left out: introduce them to one person.
- Too many people: greet, tag, point to the group, keep moving.
- Too few people: sit together, order something, treat it as a test run.
