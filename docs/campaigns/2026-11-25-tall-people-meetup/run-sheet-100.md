# 100 things to make the first Tall People Meetup work

Public Google Doc: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pkksCWZSezYyTvtR5bKaqMsKa4mFcmn5xcXRCQNcnzw/edit

Event: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 5pm to 8pm, Rosie's Red-Hot Cantina, 4 Queens Wharf, Wellington. All welcome.

This is a run sheet for the host and the helpers. It starts the night before and ends with the next meetup date.

## The ten that matter most

- Confirm the venue, the time and the reserved area.
- Put a sign at the door and one near the bar.
- Greet every person within 30 seconds.
- Offer a name tag, not a test.
- Make one short welcome at 5:30 or 6:00.
- Introduce a newcomer to one person, not the whole room.
- Take one group photo at 6:30, with consent.
- Thank the venue by name.
- Collect one piece of feedback.
- Post a follow-up and set the next date within 48 hours.

## Before: tonight, 24 November

1. Confirm the booking in writing for 5pm to 8pm.
2. Confirm the reserved area and the expected headcount with the venue.
3. Confirm step-free entry, an accessible toilet and seating.
4. Ask about ceiling height, low lights and overhead fixtures in the reserved area.
5. Confirm kitchen hours and the last food order.
6. Confirm card, EFTPOS and the split-bill policy.
7. Ask for two non-alcoholic options that are not just soft drink.
8. Ask the venue to keep music low enough for conversation.
9. Ask whether the lights can be adjusted for photos.
10. Get a staff contact name and number for the day.
11. Check the live event page says 25 November, 5pm to 8pm.
12. Fix any page or asset that still says 7pm.
13. Update the Instagram bio link to the event page.
14. Post a story with the date, time, venue and address.
15. Post a feed reminder with the same facts.
16. Message everyone who RSVP'd maybe.
17. Ask two reliable people to arrive at 4:30 as helpers.
18. Assign one greeter and one photo person.
19. Write a 30-word welcome and practice it.
20. Prepare three conversation prompts that are not about height.
21. Print a name tag sheet and bring marker pens.
22. Print two A3 signs: one for the door and one for the bar.
23. Pack tape, Blu Tack, scissors and a marker.
24. Pack a phone charger and a power bank.

26. Recheck the event page and the venue booking.
27. Post a morning story that says "Tonight".
28. Post the day-of feed image.
29. Confirm helper arrival times by message.
30. Confirm the photo policy with the photographer.
31. Pack a first-aid kit and any personal medication.
32. Pack water for yourself and the helpers.
33. Bring a cash float for tips or split bills.
34. Print the RSVP list and note who is coming alone.
35. Make a "first meetup" sticker.

## Setup, 4:15 to 4:45

41. Arrive 45 minutes early.
42. Confirm the reserved area with staff.
43. Move furniture only if the venue allows it.
44. Put the door sign at eye level.
45. Put the bar sign where people order drinks.
46. Check the sightline from the door to the group.
47. Set up the name tag table beside the entrance.
48. Keep the tag table narrow so it does not block the door.
49. Put one chair at the tag table.
50. Put water and glasses on the main table.
51. Check the floor for trip hazards and cables.
52. Check the toilets and report anything broken.
53. Ask staff to lower the music if it is loud.
54. Check the room temperature and decide how to manage the doors.
55. Brief staff on the group name and expected numbers.
56. Brief helpers on the welcome, tags and photo rules.
57. Test the QR code or feedback link.
58. Put a spare sign at the far end of the room.
59. Set a phone alarm for the group photo.
60. Take a photo of the setup for the recap.

## Arrivals, 5:00 to 5:45

61. Stand where you can see the door.
62. Greet each person within 30 seconds.
63. Ask what brought them, not how tall they are.
64. Offer a name tag with room for a name and pronouns.
65. Introduce a newcomer to one person, not the whole room.
66. Show them where to get a drink.
67. Point out the quiet corner.
68. Explain the shape of the night: 5pm to 8pm, come and go.
69. Explain the photo policy before any photo.
70. Ask whether they came alone or with someone, and welcome the mate too.
71. Watch for anyone standing at the edge.
72. Give an early arriver a small job: greet the next person.
73. Keep the first 20 minutes low-pressure.
74. Do not make a formal speech before 5:30.
75. Do not let a conversation circle close completely.

## The middle, 5:45 to 7:15

76. Make one short welcome at 5:30 or 6:00.
77. Thank the venue by name.
78. Explain the group in one sentence.
79. Ask for a show of hands: first meetup or returning.
80. Run one optional icebreaker for 10 minutes only.
81. Use prompts about tall-life logistics: travel, clothes, desks and venues.
82. Move your own position every 15 minutes.
83. Introduce people who share an interest.
84. Keep watching the door for late arrivals.
85. Take candid wide shots, not close-ups.
86. Ask before posting any face.
87. Take the group photo at 6:30.
88. Post one mid-event story if the group agrees.
89. Check that the quiet corner is still quiet.
90. Agree with staff on how to handle a disruptive guest.

## Close and follow-up, 7:15 to 8:00

91. Announce the end time at 7:30.
92. Do a last call for the group photo at 7:40.
93. Thank people individually where you can.
94. Ask one question: what should the next meetup do?
95. Collect feedback through the QR code or a one-question form.
96. Settle the bill and leave a tip.
97. Check for lost property and take down the signs.
98. Leave the space as you found it.
99. Post a thank-you story the same night.
100. Write down attendance and pick a date for the next meetup within 48 hours.

## Optional activities

Pick two. Do not run all of these at a first meetup.

- Buddy pairing: match a first-timer with one early arriver.
- Conversation cards with prompts about travel, clothes, desks and venue headroom.
- Venue bingo: headroom, legroom, coat hook, accessible toilet, quiet corner.
- A "first meetup" dot on the name tag.
- A quiet table with no standing.
- A "bring a mate" table for people who came with a friend.
- A one-question feedback card at the door.
- A next-meetup sign-up sheet.
- A venue shortlist sheet for the next event.
- A group photo at 6:30 and one wide room shot at 7:00.

## Success measures

Write these down before you sleep.

- How many RSVP'd.
- How many came.
- How many were first-timers.
- How many said they would come again.
- How many useful venue notes you collected.
- Whether the venue would host the next one.
- Whether you have one usable photo and one usable quote.

## Photo and consent rule

Ask before a close-up. Ask before posting a face. A wide room shot with no single person as the subject is usually fine. If someone says no, do not take the photo. If someone asks you to delete a photo, delete it on the spot.
