# Reel scripts

Shoot vertical on a phone. 1080 by 1920. Keep the text inside the middle of the frame so Instagram controls do not cover it.

Use a track from the Instagram library that you have rights to. Keep it low under any talking. Do not use a track you do not have rights to.

## Reel 1: tallest person

Length: 7 seconds

Shots:

1. Push in on the Rosie's door.
2. Tilt up to a tall friend.
3. Cut to the group laughing at the table.

On-screen text:

`You will not be the tallest person in the room.`

End card:

`Wed 25 Nov / 5pm to 8pm / Rosie's / 4 Queens Wharf / All welcome`

## Filming notes

- Use the back camera, not the selfie camera.
- Lock exposure before you shoot so the window light does not blow out.
- Record 2 seconds before and after each action. That gives you room to cut.
- Keep the phone still or use a small tripod. Do not add fake camera shake.
- Use the room sound if it is clean. Pub noise is part of the point.
- Do not add AI voices. Do not add fake crowd noise.
- No captions burned into the footage until the final export. Use Instagram auto captions and fix the spelling.
